# Vocabulary review — SayThrough

> **What is being asked:** confirm this vocabulary is clinically sound for
> AAC users, or tell us what to change. §19.6 makes this a release gate and
> it has **not** been cleared — nothing here has been reviewed by a clinician.

Word lists were assembled from published core vocabulary sources (Project
Core / Universal Core, AAC Language Lab, Banajee et al.) per the project's
*adapt, don't invent* rule, then filled out by a developer. That last part
is where the risk is.

A recent example of what this review is for: **no form of the verb "to be"**
existed on any board — no *am, is, are, was* — until a user tried to say "I
am hungry" and could not. An audit then found 32 high-frequency words
missing. Please assume more gaps of that kind remain.

## The boards

"Core words" means the persistent region plus the core word pages — the
vocabulary that works in any context, as opposed to topic nouns.

## Questions we need answered

### 1. Is the persistent core the right set?

These appear on **every page** at fixed positions and cannot be reached in
fewer taps. Getting this wrong costs more than anything else here.

- **5×6** (15): I, you, it, want, like, more, go, help, stop, do, feel, look, not, yes, no
- **3×4** (6): I, want, go, more, my, no
- **6×10** (24): I, you, it, me, my, that, what, where, want, need, like, have, go, help, stop, do, feel, look, more, not, done, yes, no, please

### 2. Are the vocabulary levels drawn in the right place?

A user set to Basic sees only level-1 words. Words never move between
levels — raising a level only reveals more.

- **5×6**: Basic 28, Intermediate 56, Full 97
- **3×4**: Basic 20, Intermediate 0, Full 0
- **6×10**: Basic 42, Intermediate 84, Full 363

### 3. Words with no picture symbol

These render as text only. Conventional for function words in AAC, but
please confirm none of them need a symbol — especially for pre-literate users.

`about`, `all done`, `am`, `anything`, `are`, `are you`, `away`, `can i`, `can't`, `come here`, `could`, `did`, `did you`, `didn't`, `do you`, `does`, `don't`, `each other`, `early`, `enough`, `excuse me`, `extra`, `frustrated`, `going to`, `good job`, `grumpy`, `guess what`, `had`, `has`, `how come`, `is`, `is it`, `isn't`, `just`, `let`, `little bit`, `mean`, `might`, `must`, `myself`, `need to`, `off`, `pajamas`, `pull`, `should`, `sleepy`, `something`, `soon`, `that's mine`, `their`, `want to`, `was`, `were`, `what for`, `what happened`, `what is that`, `where is it`, `which`, `who is that`, `whose`, `will you`, `won't`, `would`, `your turn`, `yourself`

### 4. Symbol choices nobody has looked at

These labels had no exact ARASAAC match, so a synonym was used and the
resulting picture has never been viewed. **Please check these on-screen.**

`describing`, `social`, `desk`, `loud`, `shorts`, `art`, `done`, `class`, `maybe`, `not`, `questions`, `little words`, `down`, `careful`, `helping words`, `pronouns`

### 5. Words whose colour changes between pages

Fitzgerald colour follows part of speech. These are coded differently on
different pages, usually because the meaning differs (a body *back* vs.
going *back*). Confirm that is right, or tell us to unify them.

- **6×10**
  - `back` — little on Little Words; noun on Body

### 6. Words that appear on more than one page

Repetition is normal in AAC — a word can belong in several places. Confirm
each of these earns its second cell rather than wasting one.

- **6×10** (21): `eat` (Actions, Food), `drink` (Actions, Drinks), `play` (Actions, Play), `listen` (Actions, Social), `never` (Helping Words, Time), `always` (Helping Words, Time), `same` (Pronouns, Describing), `another` (Pronouns, Quantity), `hot` (Describing, Weather), `cold` (Describing, Drinks, Weather), `hungry` (Feelings, Food), `thirsty` (Feelings, Drinks), `wait` (Social, Time), `again` (Social, Little Words), `together` (Social, Little Words), `my turn` (Social, Play), `your turn` (Social, Play), `back` (Little Words, Body), `empty` (Quantity, Drinks), `music` (Play, School), `library` (Places, School)
