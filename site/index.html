New to AAC?

What is AAC?

AAC — Augmentative and Alternative Communication — is any way of communicating besides talking. For people who are nonspeaking or whose speech is hard to understand, a symbol-and-word app like SayThrough becomes their voice: they tap to build a message, and the device speaks it aloud.

How it works

Three taps to a spoken sentence

Tap words & symbols

Choose from color-coded core words and picture symbols, organized for fast access.

Build the sentence

Each tap adds to the message bar so you can say a single word or a whole thought.

Press Speak

SayThrough reads it aloud in a clear voice — and highlights each word as it's spoken.

Private by default

SayThrough is local-first: your profiles, vocabulary, and messages stay on your device. There's no account and nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export a backup.

Open source

The code is public, so anyone can verify how it works and how it protects privacy — and the community can help make it better.
