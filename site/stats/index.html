What is counted

Counts, and nothing else. There is no account, no cookie, no session identifier and no device fingerprint behind these numbers — so 1,240 app opens could be 1,240 people or one person 1,240 times. Trends are meaningful; exact reach is not, and that is deliberate: counting individual people would mean identifying them, and many people using SayThrough are children with disabilities.

Nothing anyone says, types or taps inside the app is ever sent anywhere. Communication stays on the device. You can turn this counting off in the app under Settings → Privacy.
