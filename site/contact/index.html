Get in touch

SayThrough is a small, free project — messages are read by a person, not a support desk. Here's the fastest route depending on what you need.

Something is broken

Bug reports are most useful on GitHub, where they can be tracked and fixed in the open: open an issue. If you'd rather not use GitHub, email works too — please say what device and browser you were using.

Vocabulary and clinical feedback

Especially welcome from SLPs, teachers and AAC users. The starter vocabulary is a candidate set awaiting clinical review, so if a word is missing, mis-placed, or the wrong symbol, that feedback directly improves what everyone gets.

Please don't send personal information

Don't include a child's name, diagnosis, school, or anything from inside someone's board. It isn't needed to help, and this is an ordinary email inbox — not a secure or HIPAA-covered channel.
